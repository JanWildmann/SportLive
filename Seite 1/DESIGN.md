## Brand & Style
The design system pairs certified German healthcare precision with dynamic athletic vitality. Tailored for certified prevention courses (§ 20 SGB V, ZPP) and live interactive training, the aesthetic projects statutory rigor, institutional reliability, and motivational clarity. 

The design combines high-end **Corporate Modernism** with subtle clinical functionalism:
- **Atmosphere**: Crisp, clean, reassuring, compliant. Evokes institutional trust akin to statutory health insurers (Techniker, Barmer, AOK) while retaining the agility of an elite digital fitness studio.
- **Visual Weight**: Balanced, structured grids with purposeful whitespace. Zero visual gimmicks, ungrounded gradients, or decorative noise that might diminish compliance integrity.
- **Editorial Voice in UI**: Direct, articulate, transparent. Complex statutory frameworks (Impressum under § 5 DDG, DSGVO/GDPR disclosures, ZPP refund terms) receive first-class structural hierarchy alongside interactive course cards.

## Colors
The palette balances active health vitality with deep regulatory trust:
- **Primary (`#10B981` Emerald)**: Represents vitality, health insurance validation, successful certification, and active forward momentum. Reserved for core conversions, validated states, and certification badges.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6` Oceanic Sapphire)**: Conveys clinical digital infrastructure, institutional security, live stream connectivity (Zoom), and data privacy indicators. Used for technical highlights, active interactive tabs, and informational accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` Deep Slate)**: Grounded, definitive authority. Applied across primary headlines, structural frames, and the heavy statutory footer housing legal disclosures.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` Slate)**: Muted structural lines, helper text, metadata labels, and subtle divider rules.
- **Surfaces**: Ultra-crisp slate white (`#F8FAFC`) canvas with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface cards to ensure maximum contrast ratios for critical contractual reading.

## Typography
Typographic discipline is governed by two complementary typefaces:
- **Display & Headings (Manrope)**: Geometric, authoritative, balanced. Delivers immediate structural posture on editorial sections, course names, and institutional headers without feeling cold or sterile.
- **Body & Legal Texts (Public Sans)**: Engineered for institutional legibility and neutral clarity. Specifically chosen to optimize long-form German legal prose (DDG disclosures, DSGVO sub-processing clauses, ZPP refund terms) where compound nouns and dense sub-clauses demand unambiguous letter shapes and ample aperture.
- **Microcopy & Legal Subtext (`legal-clause`)**: Specifically tuned to `0.8125rem` (13px) with a strict line-height of `1.5` to ensure maximum accessibility and legal defensibility according to DIN standards without visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid maxing out at `1280px` width, framed by structured vertical rhythm:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` page margins. Legal sections can split into an asymmetric 4-column sticky anchor navigation and an 8-column continuous reader canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` page margins. Tabbed segments collapse into horizontal scrollable rails with snap alignment.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` page margins. Accordions take precedence over multi-column layouts to eliminate lateral scrolling and sustain vertical thumb-flow.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation reinforces order and focus rather than superficial realism:
- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background tint (`#F8FAFC`). Pure utility.
- **Level 1 (Card & Legal Container)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px crisp outline (`#E2E8F0`) and a diffused micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Accordion / Interactive Hover)**: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Border shifts dynamically to Emerald (`#10B981`) or Sapphire (`#3B82F6`) depending on semantic context.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Legal Header / Navigation Overlays)**: Crisp semi-opaque backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` and a solid bottom divider (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).

## Shapes
A conservative, disciplined curvature standard (`roundedness: 1`) is enforced:
- **Base Components (0.25rem / 4px)**: Checkboxes, badges, micro-tags, legal callout bars, and inline data indicators.
- **Containers & Controls (0.5rem / 8px)**: Input fields, buttons, interactive cards, accordion shells, and dialog containers.
- **Modal Dialogues & Featured Modules (0.75rem / 12px)**: Large certification callouts, preview modals, and payment reassurance badges.
- High-radii pills are deliberately prohibited for buttons and cards to preserve the serious, clinical character required for statutory health insurance partnerships.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Emerald (`#10B981`) surface, text in white (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Focused with a 2px offset ring in Sapphire (`#3B82F6`).
- **Secondary (Compliance/Verification)**: Crisp white surface, 1px border in Slate (`#CBD5E1`), text in Deep Slate (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions to Slate surface (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: No border, Slate text (`#475569`), used exclusively for legal cross-references and secondary navigational links.

### Legal Tabs & Accordions (DDG, DSGVO, ZPP AGB)
- **Legal Accordion Panels**: Enclosed in 1px `#E2E8F0` borders. Collapsed headers feature bold Manrope titles with categorical badges (e.g., `§ 5 DDG`, `Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO`, `ZPP Prüf-ID`). Expanded state introduces a soft left accent border (3px solid `#3B82F6`) to denote active reading scope.
- **Legal Tabs**: Pill-free segmented track in `#F1F5F9`. Active tab features white background, Deep Slate text, and an immediate subtle Level 1 shadow.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes (DSGVO Consent)
- **Inputs**: Crisp white surface with `#CBD5E1` borders, transitioning to `#3B82F6` on focus with zero color bleed. Helper and error text strictly positioned below field in `legal-clause` sizing.
- **Compliance Checkboxes**: Square 18x18px with `0.25rem` radius. Explicitly separated from long-form consent labels, allowing full tap target across the descriptive German consent text.

### Verification Chips & Badges
- **ZPP Certified Badge**: Soft Emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), Emerald text (`#065F46`), with a solid 1px `#A7F3D0` border.
- **DSGVO / Zoom Encryption Badge**: Soft Blue background (`#EFF6FF`), Sapphire text (`#1E40AF`), with a solid 1px `#BFDBFE` border.

### Statutory Footer
- **Footer Frame**: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) canvas with contrasting light slate typography (`#94A3B8`). Houses structured, explicit links to Impressum, Datenschutz, AGB, and Widerrufsbelehrung, flanked by official trainer credentials and certification marks.
